# Playbooks

Use this file for execution patterns after reading the research notes.

## Topic scoring rubric

Score each dimension `0-2` before drafting.

- `Timeliness`: 0 no trigger; 1 weak seasonal or background trend; 2 active public trigger.
- `Audience width`: 0 narrow expert-only; 1 adjacent audience; 2 broad relevance.
- `Credibility`: 0 generic; 1 some examples; 2 strong firsthand data, cases, or authority.
- `Emotional payoff`: 0 flat; 1 interesting; 2 relieving, empowering, clarifying, or deeply understood.
- `Discussability`: 0 no natural reaction; 1 mild; 2 readers will want to forward, save, or take a stance.

Interpretation:

- `8-10`: publishable angle.
- `6-7`: needs sharper framing.
- `0-5`: re-angle before writing.

## Title patterns

Write at least three title variants.

### Searchable

Use when the task is reach-first.

Formula:

`[topic keyword] + [specific change/problem] + [reader payoff]`

Examples:

- `延迟退休落地后，普通人最该先改的不是简历，而是这3件事`
- `AI搜索改写流量分配后，公众号标题还该怎么写`

### Emotional

Use when the task needs stronger resonance or "在看" intent.

Formula:

`[specific scene or people] + [hidden conflict] + [clear judgment or comfort]`

Examples:

- `35岁以后最难的，不是找工作，而是不敢慢下来`
- `很多人不是不会表达，只是从没被认真接住过`

### Editorial

Use when the account voice should feel calmer and more premium.

Formula:

`[event or phenomenon]： [interpretation / consequence]`

Examples:

- `流量重新分配之后：公众号正在奖励哪一种写作者`
- `一次热搜背后：为什么真正打动人的内容越来越具体`

## First-screen hook patterns

Choose one.

### Contradiction hook

State what most people think, then overturn it.

Template:

`很多人以为 [common belief]，但真正决定 [outcome] 的，是 [unexpected factor]。`

### Scene hook

Open with a concrete moment.

Template:

`[time / place / role] 的那一刻，真正暴露出来的不是 [surface issue]，而是 [deeper issue]。`

### Cost hook

Show what the reader loses by ignoring the issue.

Template:

`这件事最容易被低估的，不是 [surface issue]，而是它正在悄悄改变 [reader consequence]。`

### Recognition hook

Make the reader feel understood.

Template:

`如果你最近总觉得 [emotion / frustration]，问题可能不在你不够努力，而在 [reframe]。`

## Body structures

### Hot-topic commentary

Use for policy, AI, market, public events.

1. State the event and the overlooked question.
2. Explain why it matters to the target reader now.
3. Offer your judgment, not a timeline dump.
4. End with a practical implication or stance.

### Emotional resonance

Use for culture, relationship, burnout, identity, social mood.

1. Open with a familiar scene.
2. Name the conflict precisely.
3. Reframe the conflict in a kinder or sharper way.
4. Close with a sentence worth saving or forwarding.

### Practical guide

Use for method posts.

1. Name the common mistake.
2. Explain why it happens.
3. Give a small set of actions.
4. Close with a checklist.

### Narrative / non-fiction

Use for人物、事件、深度写作.

1. Introduce a person or turning point quickly.
2. Expand into structure or system.
3. Keep specific scenes throughout.
4. End on meaning, not ornament.

## CTA patterns for likes and shares

Prefer these over generic begging:

- `Question CTA`: ask a real either-or question.
- `Identity CTA`: invite readers to stand with a viewpoint they feel proud to endorse.
- `Utility CTA`: offer a short checklist worth saving.
- `Relay CTA`: end with a sentence the reader would forward to one person immediately.

Examples:

- `如果是你，你会更在意稳定，还是更在意还能不能继续相信自己？`
- `这不是一句鸡汤，而是很多人此刻最需要被允许的一件事。`
- `如果你身边也有人正卡在这一步，这篇可以先留着。`

## Output template

Use this shape unless the user asks for a different format.

```markdown
目标读者:
主目标:
  - 高阅读 / 高点赞 / 平衡

共性判断:
  - 为什么这个题有机会被打开
  - 为什么这个题有人愿意点赞/在看

文章角度:
  - 一句话 thesis

标题:
  - 版本 A
  - 版本 B
  - 版本 C

首屏:
  - 2-4 句

结构:
  - Part 1
  - Part 2
  - Part 3

结尾 CTA:
  - 推荐写法

风险:
  - 可能导致打开率下降的问题
  - 可能导致点赞率下降的问题
```
