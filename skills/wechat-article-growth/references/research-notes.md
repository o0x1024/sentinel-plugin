# Research Notes

Use this file when the task needs source-backed reasoning instead of generic content advice.

## Stable findings

### 1. Recommendation traffic and originality matter more than they used to

- Newrank's 2024 WeChat recap says public accounts produced more than `30.78万` 10w+ articles in 2024, including `11.87万` original 10w+ articles. It also notes that recommendation traffic can dominate total reads; for Newrank's own account, `推荐` accounted for `59.2%` of total reads in 2024.
- The same article says originality is more likely to receive platform recommendation and that small or unexpected topics can also break out under recommendation logic.
- Source: [新榜，微信2024复盘](https://www.newrank.cn/article/detail/29573)

Implication:

- High-read articles are no longer only "big account + hot headline".
- Original framing and recommendation suitability matter.
- Searchability and recommendation compatibility should be designed into the title and opening.

### 2. Hot topics still win, but not every winner is a pure news account

- Newrank's 2024 recap says the 2024公众号 baseline was still dominated by `时事新闻` and `情感文化`.
- It also highlights `AI` and `非虚构` as rising breakout directions.
- Source: [新榜，微信2024复盘](https://www.newrank.cn/article/detail/29573)

Implication:

- A strong angle often sits at the intersection of `current event x broad emotion x specific insight`.
- Pure information is weaker than "what this event means for me / us".

### 3. High-read titles are not random

- A study summary on high-read WeChat titles says titles usually combine elements such as `热点 + 数字 + 领袖名人 + 相关主题关键词`.
- It also says nouns, verbs, numerals, and pronouns dominate; effect is strongest in the first three days after publication and usually fades within a week.
- The study further notes title length should be controlled by topic rather than stretched arbitrarily.
- Source: [《微信公众号高阅读量推文标题特征研究》摘要转引](https://m.fx361.com/news/2021/0910/9632030.html)

Implication:

- Title writing should optimize for scannability and compression.
- A title should expose topic, stake, and payoff quickly instead of reading like an essay sentence.

### 4. Like behavior is driven more by trust and positive emotion than by raw information density

- A 2021 paper on WeChat user engagement finds that `信息质量` and `来源可信度` influence like intention more strongly than comment intention.
- It also finds `正向情绪` and `来源可信度` are stronger than information quality for both like and comment intention, while `负向情绪` hurts like intention.
- Source: [管理科学学报：在线用户点赞与评论行为的产生机理差异研究](https://jmsc.tju.edu.cn/jmsc/article/abstract/20210404?st=search)

Implication:

- If the user wants likes or "在看", writing should not rely only on information density.
- The article needs trust signals, emotional recognition, and a tone readers are willing to publicly endorse.

### 5. Original content has shown durable advantage over time

- Newrank's 2019 annual report says average single-article reads were falling overall, but original content retained stronger vitality and its average like performance kept improving relative to earlier years.
- Source: [2019中国微信500强年报 | 新榜](https://www.newrank.cn/article/detail/13079?tag=%E5%B9%B4%E6%8A%A5)

Implication:

- For medium-to-long-term account growth, copying topic shells is insufficient.
- Distinct voice, perspective, and firsthand synthesis matter.

## Pattern synthesis

Treat the points below as inference from the sources above rather than direct platform claims.

### High-read commonalities

- They attach to a clear social trigger: news event, policy change, industry shift, or widely felt problem.
- They surface a narrow angle instead of rehashing the entire topic.
- They communicate payoff fast in title and first screen.
- They remain legible to a broad audience even when the subject is niche.
- They are easy for recommendation systems and users to classify quickly.

### High-like commonalities

- They feel safe and rewarding to endorse publicly.
- They contain specific credibility signals: cases, firsthand observation, named actors, evidence, or strong editorial judgment.
- They create recognition rather than vague agitation.
- They usually end on a sentence or question the reader can "stand behind".

### High-read and high-like overlap

- Timely topic.
- Broad emotional relevance.
- Clear stance or insight.
- Strong first-screen hook.
- Memorable closing sentence.

## What not to over-generalize

- Do not assume every爆文 must chase breaking news. Evergreen practical content can still work if the benefit is immediate and visible.
- Do not assume controversy automatically increases likes. Negative emotional load can suppress endorsement.
- Do not assume long-form depth hurts performance. Non-fiction and deep analysis still work when the opening justifies the read.
